02How I Work

A process built for
clarity and craft

Good design process isn't linear — it's a series of informed decisions. These are the phases I move through, and how I think within each one. The emphasis shifts by project, but the rigour stays constant.

01Discover

I start by understanding the problem space, not assuming I already know it.

  • Stakeholder interviews & alignment workshops
  • User research: contextual inquiry, depth interviews
  • Competitive landscape & heuristic audits
  • Analytics review and funnel analysis
02Define

Turn fuzzy signals into a sharp problem statement and testable hypotheses.

  • Synthesis: affinity mapping, Jobs-to-be-Done framing
  • Opportunity trees and “How Might We” statements
  • Success metrics defined before design starts
  • Scope negotiation with product and engineering
03Design

Explore broadly, then converge on the most effective solution.

  • Low-fi sketching and rapid concept exploration
  • Interactive prototypes in Figma
  • Usability testing — 5+ participants per round
  • Design critique and structured feedback loops
04Deliver

Ship, measure, and improve. Design doesn't end at handoff.

  • Annotated specs and dev-ready component docs
  • Implementation QA and pixel-level review
  • Post-launch metrics review against success criteria
  • Iteration roadmap based on observed behavior
