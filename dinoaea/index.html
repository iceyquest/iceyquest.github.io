DIONAEA

Born hungry. Grow from a tiny flytrap into a towering nightmare. Aim with your mouse and CLICK to lash out and devour prey. Press SPACE to fire poisonous quills at approaching threats. At the final evolution, hunting drains the forest. Catch glowing golden BEES to scatter seeds and restore the land before it dies.

Mouse / touch to aim • Click / tap to bite • Space / QUILL button to defend

MOVE: A / D or ← / →   BITE: Click / Tap   QUILLS: Space   PAUSE: P
TIP: Click near flying prey to strike with your tongue. Quills travel toward your cursor. At higher evolutions, your jaws can reach bigger prey. Golden striped BEES restore the forest when eaten. In the final form, hunting dries the land; if FOREST reaches zero, you perish. Survive chainsaw hunters.
